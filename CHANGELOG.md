# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22

First public release.

- Radial wheel: hold Shift while dragging a file, on macOS and Windows.
- Four pages:
  - **Image formats:** PNG, WEBP, HEIC, GIF, AVIF, JPEG, PDF, DOCX, TIFF.
  - **Tools:** compress, strip metadata, remove/add background, edit, annotate, crop, redact, preview.
  - **Video & audio:** MP4, GIF, MP3, WAV, MOV, WEBM, M4A, compress, mute.
  - **PDF & documents:** to PDF, compress, split, merge, OCR, to images, to Word, print.
- Converter window for many files at once, plus conversion from the clipboard.
- Notifications with Open, Show in folder and Undo (moves the result to the Trash or Recycle Bin).
- Five themes. Choice of output folder and quality.
- Command line: `DragWheel --run <action> <files…>`.
